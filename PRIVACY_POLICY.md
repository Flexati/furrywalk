## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Account Data
- **OAuth identifiers** (Google/Apple sign-in tokens) for authentication
- **Email address** associated with your sign-in provider

### 2.2 Dog Profile Data
- Dog name, breed, age, weight, photo (optional)
- Health records: vaccination dates, anti-parasite treatments, vet contacts

### 2.3 Walk Data
- GPS location tracks during active walks
- Walk duration, distance, route geometry
- Photos taken during walks

### 2.4 Usage Data
- App interaction analytics (anonymous)
- Crash reports (via Expo)

### 2.5 Payment Data
- Subscription tier and status (Free / Pro / Pro Family)
- Purchase tokens and order IDs (Google Play Billing)
- **We do NOT store full credit card details.** Payments are processed by Google Play.

## 3. How We Use Your Data

- **Walk tracking:** GPS data is used solely to record your walking route and calculate distance. GPS is only active during an actively tracked walk.
- **Dog health:** Reminder system for vaccinations and anti-parasite treatments.
- **Subscription:** To manage your Pro subscription and ad-free experience.
- **Improvement:** Anonymous analytics help us improve the app.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do **NOT** sell your data to third parties. Data is shared only with:

| Service | Purpose | Data Shared |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| Supabase | Database hosting | All app data |
| Google Play Billing | Payment processing | Purchase tokens |
| Vercel | API hosting | Encrypted in transit |

## 5. Data Retention

- **Walk data:** Retained until you delete it or delete your account.
- **GPS tracks:** Stored for your personal history. You can delete individual walks at any time.
- **Account:** Deleted upon your request (email amzajaguar@gmail.com).

## 6. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR), you have the right to:
- **Access:** Request a copy of your data
- **Rectification:** Correct inaccurate data
- **Erasure:** Delete your account and all associated data
- **Portability:** Receive your data in a machine-readable format
- **Objection:** Object to processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, email: amzajaguar@gmail.com

## 7. Location Data (Specific)

Passeggiata Furba uses foreground GPS location tracking **only during active walks** that you explicitly start. Location data:
- Is **never** collected in the background
- Is used solely to display your route on the map and calculate distance
- Can be deleted by removing the walk from your history
- Is **not** shared with any third party for advertising or analytics

## 8. Children's Privacy

Passeggiata Furba is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
